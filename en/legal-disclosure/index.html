Ordering and service timing

Customers contact SHENLAN through the website or WhatsApp to confirm the service and its price. Services are provided on the date and at the time agreed for the booking. Customers pay SHENLAN directly.

Prices and payment

Prices include tax. A deposit of one-third of the total booking price is payable in advance. You may pay the deposit while the requested slot is available. The remaining two-thirds is payable at the end of the activity.

Accepted payment methods are Japanese bank transfer and Wise.

Customer cancellation and refunds

Customer cancellation timeCancellation charge / refund
72 hours or more before the scheduled activity startNo cancellation charge. Payments received are refunded, less actual bank or Wise refund fees.
Less than 72 hours before the scheduled activity startA cancellation charge of 50% of the total booking price applies. Any payment above that charge is refunded, less actual bank or Wise refund fees. If payments received are below that charge, the customer must pay the shortfall.
At or after the scheduled activity startPayments already made are not refundable.

Refunds are returned through the original payment method. Only actual bank or Wise refund fees are deducted. See the Cancellation & Refund Policy for the calculation example and contact details.
