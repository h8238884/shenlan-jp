About the photographer

Remember the place.
And who you were in it.

I’m a Tokyo-based photographer and licensed professional driver. Bringing transport and photography together means you can enjoy your day in Japan without constantly switching between getting somewhere and taking photographs.

My approach is clean, natural and a little cinematic. I’m drawn to the look towards a distant mountain, the smile when someone steps closer, and the small moments that were never planned for the camera.

01 / THE WAY I SEE

The landscape is the setting. You are the story.

A photograph can hold a beautiful view—and the feeling of being there.

01

Room to be yourself

Walk, talk, or pause to take in the view. Photography can be part of the journey, with space for expressions that come naturally.

02

A conversation with light

Morning by the lake, shade on a side street, neon after dark. We choose settings around the season and the mood you want to remember.

03

A little room in the day

A meaningful day is not a checklist of locations. Distance, weather and crowds help shape a pace that leaves time to stop.

02 / YOUR DAY, TOGETHER

It begins with a conversation.

  1. 01

    Tell me what you have in mind

    Share your date, group size, places and preferred style. Couples, proposals, light wedding shoots, honeymoons and family trips can all begin with your own story.

  2. 02

    Shape the journey together

    We discuss the route, transport and any extras around the season, starting point and shoot. Scope, pricing and availability are confirmed before booking.

  3. 03

    Step into the scenery

    The order of the day adapts to light, weather, visibility and crowds. Transport and photography are coordinated together, with fewer arrangements for you to juggle.
