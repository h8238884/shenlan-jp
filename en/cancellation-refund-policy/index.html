Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Customer cancellation of a direct booking

The following schedule applies when a customer cancels an activity booked directly with SHENLAN. The cutoff is measured from the scheduled activity start time.

Customer cancellation timeCancellation charge / refund
72 hours or more before the scheduled activity startNo cancellation charge. Payments received are refunded, less actual bank or Wise refund fees.
Less than 72 hours before the scheduled activity startA cancellation charge of 50% of the total booking price applies. Any payment above that charge is refunded, less actual bank or Wise refund fees. If payments received are below that charge, the customer must pay the shortfall.
At or after the scheduled activity startPayments already made are not refundable.

How the 50% cancellation charge works

The charge is calculated from the total booking price, not just the deposit.

For example, for a booking priced at ¥90,000, the cancellation charge is ¥45,000. If only the ¥30,000 deposit has been paid, a further ¥15,000 is payable. If the full ¥90,000 has been paid, ¥45,000 is refundable before deducting actual bank or Wise refund fees.

Refund method and fees

Refunds are returned through the original payment method. Only the actual fees charged by the bank or Wise for the refund are deducted.
