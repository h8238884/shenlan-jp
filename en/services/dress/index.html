Before you rent

Can I request a dress shown here?

Use its reference number to share your preference. The actual dress, size and availability must be confirmed with the studio.

Can I take a dress to a Fuji or Tokyo photoshoot?

Share the locations, route and expected duration. Outdoor use, collection, return and any delivery arrangements need individual confirmation.

How are prices and accessories confirmed?

Once you have a dress and date in mind, confirm the quote and inclusions. Accessories, cleaning and any deposit requirements are agreed before booking.
