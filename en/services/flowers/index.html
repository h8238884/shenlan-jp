Ordering and handover

Share a reference, palette, budget, occasion and any flowers to include or avoid. Confirm the date, handover location and method before production. The finished arrangement cannot be changed.

Can I choose exact flowers or stem counts, or copy a photo?

Florist’s-choice bouquets are original designs based on your selected palette and that day’s flowers. Specific flower varieties cannot be requested for this option, and quantities vary. Photos are style references rather than exact templates. Discuss any flowers to avoid before production.

Can a completed bouquet be changed?

Once a bouquet has been made to the agreed brief, it cannot be revised. Please discuss your palette, style, occasion and special requests in advance. If you need a strictly specified design, enquire separately about availability.

How are delivery, collection and costs arranged?

Please share the date, handover location and photoshoot schedule. We will confirm available arrangements and the final cost during your enquiry. Only flowers explicitly listed in your agreed photoshoot quote are included.
