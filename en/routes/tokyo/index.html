Tokyo (Custom Photoshoot)

From shrine calm to neon night. We design the route by season, crowds, and your style (clean / cinematic / street).

Three ways to photograph Tokyo

01WEST TOKYO · CITY WALKS

An ordinary day, a little cinematic.

Follow the shade of shrine trees into the rhythm of the streets. Let the city move around you.

Photo locations

  • Morning calm at Meiji Shrine & forest paths
  • Harajuku Takeshita Street (pop / kawaii)
  • Cat Street walk (boutiques + cafes)
  • Hachiko + Shibuya Scramble Crossing (iconic)
  • Optional: Shibuya Sky sunset (if you book tickets)
02ASAKUSA · OLD TOKYO

Take the slower street.

Lanterns, temple gates and a breeze from the river: small details that become your memories of Tokyo.

Photo locations

  • Kaminarimon gate & Senso-ji area
  • Nakamise street (snacks / souvenirs)
  • Backstreets for cleaner portraits
  • Sumida River / Skytree in background
03TOKYO · AFTER DARK

Stay for the lights.

Neon traces a silhouette; rain leaves reflections behind. Let the night set the scene for your story.

Photo locations

  • Kabukicho neon + cinematic frames
  • Omoide Yokocho / small alleys for mood
  • Street-light portraits (film look)
  • Alternative: Ginza / Tokyo Station night vibe

Best for

  • 01Couples, solo, friends, family
  • 02Kimono / casual street / proposal
  • 03First-time Tokyo visitors

Notes

  • 01Timing matters: we avoid peak crowds and chase the best light.
  • 02We can start from your hotel (central Tokyo) or meet at the first location.
  • 03If it rains: we use covered arcades / reflections for a stronger cinematic look.
