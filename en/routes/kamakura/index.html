Kamakura & Enoshima (Custom)

Temples, Enoden, and sea breeze. A slower-paced photoshoot with coastal mood.

FIND YOUR PACE

A setting for your own story.

Locations are ideas for your shoot. The final selection and order depend on weather, light and crowds.

01KAMAKURA · OLD TOWN

Let the old town set the pace.

From lively Komachi-dori to quieter lanes, find a smile between the shrine gates and stone steps.

Photo locations

  • Komachi-dori street (snacks / casual portraits)
  • Tsurugaoka Hachimangu (grand stairs + shrine vibe)
  • Quiet backstreets for cleaner frames
02HASE · TEMPLES & FLOWERS

Pause where the flowers grow.

Wander past temple gardens and seasonal blooms, leaving room for the moments that happen between poses.

Photo locations

  • Iconic Great Buddha shot (timing matters)
  • Hasedera temple views + seasonal flowers
  • Short walks with soft light (great for couples)
03COAST · ENODEN

Follow the train towards the sea.

A breeze, a passing train, the last light on the water. Keep a little of the coast with you.

Photo locations

  • Enoden atmosphere (retro local train)
  • Coastline portraits + wind + silhouettes
  • Optional: Enoshima island for golden hour

Best for

  • 01Couples / proposals
  • 02Family day trip from Tokyo
  • 03Film-like seaside portraits

Notes

  • 01Weekends get crowded: we adjust order and timing to avoid the worst rush.
  • 02If visibility is good, we can frame Mt. Fuji from the coast at sunset.
  • 03Route can be split: “temples-focused” or “sea-focused”.
