Mt. Fuji field notes

South · Town streetsYume Bridge · Kembori Station

  • Yume Bridge

    Strong leading lines for an impactful “face-to-Fuji” composition. Placing the subject slightly off-center feels more natural and premium.

  • Kembori Station

    A small, quiet station with a very Japanese vibe. Great for slow, story-like moments: waiting, walking the platform, a shy smile.

  • Fuji Station West Exit

    Feels like a good “closing scene” for the trip. Keep the composition clean and add small actions for a more lived-in feel.

  • Hikawa Clock Shop

    Deep street perspective gives strong leading lines and a surprise Fuji reveal. Great for city-story moments: walking, waiting at lights, looking back.

  • Mt. Fuji Station

    Lots of elements to create “departure/arrival” feelings. Perfect for everyday interactions: drinks in hand, fixing hair/clothes, pre-ride moments.

  • Lake Yamanaka (Hiranohama)

    Lake and sky create a relaxed, romantic mood. With good light, the background layers are very clean and breathable.

North · Lakes and highlandsAsagiri Plateau · Saiko lakeside

  • Asagiri Plateau

    Open fields and a clean horizon make the “person + Fuji” ratio easy to control. Minimal yet not empty; windy days feel cinematic.

  • Saiko lakeside

    Water softens the light for a gentler look. Great for quiet emotions: walking, hugging, glancing back at the mountain.

  • Nagasaki Park

    Many angles in one area. Easy to switch compositions: subject foreground, lakeshore mid-ground, Fuji in the background.

  • Kawaguchiko Lawson

    A signature “Fuji + street” landmark. Keep composition and tones restrained to avoid a tourist look; elevate with pose and details.

  • Hikawa Clock Shop

    Deep street perspective gives strong leading lines and a surprise Fuji reveal. Great for city-story moments: walking, waiting at lights, looking back.

  • Mt. Fuji Station

    Lots of elements to create “departure/arrival” feelings. Perfect for everyday interactions: drinks in hand, fixing hair/clothes, pre-ride moments.

  • Lake Yamanaka (Hiranohama)

    Lake and sky create a relaxed, romantic mood. With good light, the background layers are very clean and breathable.

Detours · Parks and shrinesKawaguchiko Asama Shrine · Arakurayama Sengen Shrine

  • Kawaguchiko Asama Shrine

    A quiet, everyday shrine atmosphere—low-key but story-rich.

  • Arakurayama Sengen Shrine

    Iconic viewpoint with strong layers (architecture / steps / Fuji), perfect for a set with “travel memory” vibes.

  • Oishi Park

    Very seasonal. Flowers + lake make the frame softer and more “Japan-like”. Great for relaxed, natural interactions.
