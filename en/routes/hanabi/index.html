A full evening from kimono styling to hotel drop-off

Fireworks days usually involve heavy crowds and temporary traffic restrictions, so the route is planned around a Tokyo pickup and drop-off flow. After kimono styling and makeup are completed, we depart from the kimono shop and arrange street portraits, blue-hour night scenes, and fireworks-background shots according to venue access, light, and crowd conditions before returning you to your hotel after the show.

Service flow
  • Pickup from your Tokyo hotel or kimono shop
  • Depart after kimono, hair, and makeup are ready
  • Street portraits, blue-hour night scenes, and fireworks mood
  • Hotel drop-off after the fireworks finish
Transport options
  • Private car shoot: best for couples, families, or small friend groups
  • Shared car shoot: up to 5 guests, a lighter option with pickup included
  • Tokyo, Yokohama, Kamakura, Atami, and nearby routes are planned around event traffic
Booking notes
  • Ask 2-4 weeks ahead if possible; popular fireworks days get very crowded.
  • If rain, strong wind, or cancellation happens, we can discuss rescheduling or switching to a night portrait route.
  • Kimono shop location, hotel area, guest count, and private/shared car choice affect timing and pricing.
